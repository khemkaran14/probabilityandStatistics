Chance – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Chance ("the app") teaches probability and statistics with animated lessons. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: without signing in, nothing leaves your device. If you choose to sign in, your name, email and learning progress are stored in your account so you can get them back on another phone. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used to track you.

Information the app stores

The app saves your learning progress on your device: XP, level, finished lessons, quiz results, daily goal, streak, settings (such as the reminder time and sound on/off) and whether you have unlocked premium. Uninstalling the app or using Settings → Reset progress deletes it from the device.

The app has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Optional account (sign in with Google)

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Google, the app uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, run by Google) to store:

  • your Google account's name, email address and a user ID, to identify your account;
  • your learning progress: XP, finished lessons, quiz results, streak, daily-challenge count and daily XP for the last two months.

This is used only to keep your progress safe and bring it back when you sign in on another device. Settings and your purchase are not stored in your account. The data is encrypted in transit, can be read only by your signed-in account, and is not shared with anyone else. Google's role is covered by the Firebase privacy information.

Deleting your data: in the app, open Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and the progress stored in it straight away. You can also ask for deletion by emailing the contact address below. Signing out keeps your account but stops syncing from that device.

Purchases

The optional "Unlock everything" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. The app never sees your payment details. The store tells the app only whether the purchase succeeded, so it can unlock the lessons. The store's own privacy policy covers your payment: Google · Apple.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your device at the time you choose. No server is involved. You can turn it off in the app's settings or your phone's settings.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app opens your phone's share sheet with a short text message. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share with.

Children

The app is suitable for students. Without signing in it collects no personal information from anyone. Sign-in is intended for users aged 13 and over.
