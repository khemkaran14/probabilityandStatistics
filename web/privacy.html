Chance – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Chance ("the app") teaches probability and statistics with animated lessons. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data.

Information the app stores

The app saves your learning progress only on your device: XP, level, finished lessons, quiz results, daily goal, streak, settings (such as the reminder time and sound on/off) and whether you have unlocked premium. This data never leaves your device, and the developer cannot see it. Uninstalling the app or using Settings → Reset progress deletes it.

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Purchases

The optional "Unlock everything" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. The app never sees your payment details. The store tells the app only whether the purchase succeeded, so it can unlock the lessons. The store's own privacy policy covers your payment: Google · Apple.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your device at the time you choose. No server is involved. You can turn it off in the app's settings or your phone's settings.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app opens your phone's share sheet with a short text message. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share with.

Children

The app is suitable for students. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
