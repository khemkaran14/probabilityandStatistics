Privacy Policy – Chance: Probability & Stats

Developer: Divyrs App: Chance: Probability & Stats (Android package com.divyrs.chance) Effective date: 27 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Chance: Probability & Stats app ("Chance", "the app") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Chance is developed by Divyrs ("we", "us"). By using the app, you agree to this policy.

In short: to use Chance you create a free account with Google and add your mobile number. We use your details to run your account, save your progress and, only if you agree, to send you offers and updates. We never sell your data, the app has no ads and no tracking, and you can delete your account at any time.

1. Information we collect

When you create your account

  • Name, email address and profile photo from your Google account (when you choose "Continue with Google").
  • Mobile number you enter. We may verify it by sending a one-time code (OTP) by SMS.
  • Profile photo you choose to upload instead of your Google photo (optional, where the app offers it).
  • Your choice about offers: whether you agreed to receive offers and updates, and when you agreed or withdrew.

When you use the app

  • Learning progress: XP, finished lessons, quiz results, streak, daily-challenge count and daily XP for the last two months.

Stored only on your device (never sent to us): settings such as the daily reminder time, sound effects and vibration.

We do not collect your location, contacts, messages, files (other than a photo you choose), advertising IDs or payment card details.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in on any phone.
  • To check that your mobile number is valid and, where offered, verify by SMS that it is yours.
  • To save your progress and restore it on a new or reinstalled phone.
  • To show your name and photo in the app.
  • To answer your support requests.
  • Offers and updates (only with your consent): if you tick "Send me offers and updates from Divyrs", we may contact you by email, SMS or WhatsApp about new lessons, features and offers for Divyrs products. You can withdraw at any time in the app (Profile → untick the box) or by replying to any message or emailing us. We do not contact users who have not agreed.

3. Where your information is stored

Your account and data are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage), operated by Google, in data centres that may be located in India or other countries. Data is encrypted in transit and access is restricted by security rules: each account can read only its own data. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for their own purposes. It is processed only by the service providers that run the app for us:

  • Google Firebase: accounts, phone verification (SMS), database and photo storage.
  • Google Play: purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details; Google tells the app only whether a purchase succeeded. See https://policies.google.com/privacy.

We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your device. No server is involved. You can turn it off in the app's Settings or your phone's settings.

6. Data retention

We keep your account information and progress while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, photo and progress are deleted immediately from our database and storage (backups, if any, are removed within 30 days).

7. Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: Profile (tap your photo) → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, photo and saved progress.
  • By email: write to YOUR_PUBLIC_EMAIL from the email address of your account and ask us to delete it. We will delete your account and its data within 30 days and confirm by email.

You can also ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it at the same address.

8. Children's privacy

Chance is an educational app intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent for offers, and raise a complaint with us. Contact us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date, and significant changes will be shown in the app or its release notes.
