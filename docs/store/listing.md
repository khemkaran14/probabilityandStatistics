## Full description (4,000 characters)

```
See why probability and statistics work, not just the formulas.

Chance teaches probability and statistics through short animated lessons. Every lesson follows the same simple loop:

• WATCH an experiment run live: dice, coins, darts, polls and bell curves
• UNDERSTAND one big idea, with the formula updating as the animation plays
• ASK "why does this work?" and get the reasoning in plain language
• PLAY with the experiment: tilt the odds, add data, change the sample size
• CHECK yourself with a 3-question quiz

90 LESSONS, 17 CHAPTERS
1. Data & averages: mean, median, mode, spread, percentiles, outliers, z-scores
2. Seeing data: histograms, box plots, scatter plots, and how graphs lie
3. Probability basics: events, complements, Venn diagrams
4. Counting: permutations, combinations, the birthday paradox
5. Conditional probability: tree diagrams, independence, Bayes' theorem
6. Random variables: expected value, variance, linearity
7. Discrete distributions: binomial (the Galton board), geometric, Poisson
8. Continuous distributions: the normal curve, 68-95-99.7, exponential, t, chi-square
9. Two variables: covariance, correlation, correlation vs causation
10. The big theorems: Law of Large Numbers, Central Limit Theorem, Chebyshev
11. Sampling: methods, bias, sampling distributions, standard error
12. Estimation: maximum likelihood, confidence intervals, margin of error
13. Hypothesis testing: p-values, errors, power, z-tests, t-tests, ANOVA
14. Regression: least squares, residuals, R², logistic regression
15. Bayesian thinking: priors and posteriors, updating with evidence
16. Stats in the real world: A/B testing, Monte Carlo, Markov chains, AI
17. Paradoxes & puzzles: Monty Hall, Simpson's paradox, the gambler's fallacy

BUILT FOR LEARNING
• Daily goal, streaks and XP to keep you going
• Optional daily reminder, skipped on days you've already practised
• Daily challenge: 5 fresh questions every day
• Practice mode mixing questions from lessons you've finished
• 20+ badges to earn
• Works on phones and tablets

GREAT FOR
High-school and university students, exam preparation (AP Statistics, A-level, JEE, GRE, CFA), data science and machine-learning beginners, and anyone who wants to finally understand the numbers in the news.

FREE TO START
Chapters 1–3 and three preview lessons (the Galton board, Monty Hall and Bayes' theorem) are free. Unlock all 90 lessons with a single one-time purchase. No subscription, no ads, no account.

PRIVATE
Your progress stays on your device. No sign-in, no tracking, no ads.
```
